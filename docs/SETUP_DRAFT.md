## Part 1 — Create your Meta app

### Step 1.1 — Create the Meta app

<!-- APPROVED VERBATIM 2026-08-09. No screenshot needed for this step. -->

1. Go to **[developers.facebook.com](https://developers.facebook.com)** and log in with your Facebook account.
2. Top-right → **My Apps**.
3. Click the green **Create app** button.

Then on the wizard:

4. **App name**: `chatmany` — any name works, nobody but you sees it.
5. Check the contact email is filled in.
6. Click **Next**.
